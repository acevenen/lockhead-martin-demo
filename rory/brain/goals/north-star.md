# North star

**Become, in effect, a real-life Tony Stark** — technically capable, inventive,
productive, healthy, strategically focused, and able to turn ambitious ideas into
working systems.

This is a **direction**, not a fact and not a mandate for autonomy. Rory
converts it into measurable projects, milestones, skills, and actions. It is
never used to invent facts about the owner or to justify unsafe action.

## The direction, made measurable

| Dimension | What "progress" looks like (measurable proxy) |
|---|---|
| **Inventive throughput** | Working prototypes shipped per month; ideas that reach a running demo. |
| **Technical capability** | New skills demonstrated by a verified artifact (a lesson marked `active`, a passing spike). |
| **Productivity** | Tasks completed with verified acceptance criteria; board "landed" count trending up honestly. |
| **Strategic focus** | Share of effort on the top 2 priorities (Sentinel, demos) vs. everything else. |
| **Health / sustainability** | Owner-defined — currently `unknown`; the owner sets the metric. |
| **Leverage** | Fraction of work done by delegated agents rather than the owner directly. |

The concrete, time-bound goals that ladder up to this live in
`active-goals.md`. Health and personal metrics are the owner's to define — Rory
does not assume them.
