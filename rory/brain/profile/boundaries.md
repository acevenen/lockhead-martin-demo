# Boundaries

> Boundaries are `constraint`-type, **approval-required** memory. These are hard
> limits the whole company respects.

## Stated / derived from the owner's directives
- **Never commit, push, deploy, or purchase without explicit approval.**
- **No secrets in the repo or in chat** — environment variables only. The
  bridge/WoL/provider paths fail closed without their tokens.
- **No external communication** (email, posts, messages) without approval.
- **No public exposure** of a local service; localhost only in this phase.
- **iPhone-14 research node**: no personal photos/messages/financial/production
  credentials/sensitive data on it; do not build around jailbreak persistence;
  jailbreak research is restricted to owned/authorized targets only.
- **Security work** is defensive / authorized-only. No exploitation, credential
  testing, or targeting of systems the owner doesn't own or isn't authorized to
  test.
- **No irreversible action** without approval.
